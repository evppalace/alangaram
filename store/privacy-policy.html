OneByte Production – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

OneByte Production ("the app") is an internal work app of Kalyana Sapadu / EVP Rajeshwari Marriage Palace, 4/121 Trunk Road, Poonamallee, Chennai – 600056. It is used by our staff and by corporate clients (aggregators) to run food production, dispatch, attendance and communication. Contact: operations@kalyanasapadu.com, +91 96969 62666.

Information we collect

  • Account details – name, username, role and assigned site, created by our administrator.
  • Location – only for signed-in staff: while on a delivery trip and while signed in to attendance, including in the background, to record trip routes, arrival times and attendance location. It is not collected when you are signed out, and is never used for advertising.
  • Photos and voice messages – pictures of food, weighing and quality checks, and photos or voice notes sent in chat, taken or selected by you.
  • Work records – meal plans, production, stock, attendance, trips, ratings, escalations and chat messages.
  • Device push token – to send work alerts and chat notifications.

How we use it

Only to operate our catering and food-supply business: scheduling, safety and quality records, attendance, delivery tracking, client reporting and internal messaging. We do not sell personal data and do not show ads.

Sharing

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Google LLC) under our account. Client users see only the service data for their own sites (live status, ratings, reports). Staff location is visible to our managers and administrators, not to clients. We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the business relationship continues. Staff or clients can ask us to delete their account and personal data by emailing operations@kalyanasapadu.com; we act within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is limited by role and login.

Children

The app is for working adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
